        What the robot doesn't know (spoiler)

        The planner is told the spec: 1.5 kg payload and a 0.16 m gripper opening. In this simulation the real limits are 1.0 kg, because the wrist torque limit trips first, and 0.13 m, because wider objects slip during the lift. The grey bin also only holds two items, which the planner is never told. The only way to find these out is to try and fail.

            What the checks changed
              

              

              Practice lessons are saved to your account when the page runs inside Claude, otherwise in this browser. The arm is a concept module and is not yet in the 618-part list. Commands are planned by Claude through your Claude account when this page is opened in Claude, otherwise by a simple keyword parser; objects come from a simulated detector that knows every object's true position. The ROS 2 topics are generated in the browser from the simulation; no ROS graph is running. Joint efforts come from a quasi-static leg model and IMU and contact readings include synthetic noise. This is a design study. Off-the-shelf parts name real products; custom parts are specified by material, process and key dimensions rather than released as manufacturing drawings. Confirm ratings against current datasheets before ordering anything. The simulation runs an A* planner on a 10 cm costmap in your browser, with terrain costs, a 0.14 m step limit, and new obstacles found only through simulated lidar returns.
